Zewditu is delivered to businesses across Norway. The same coffee, the same hand-stamped roast date as in the web shop — just in the volume you need.

Price is agreed per customer and depends on quantity and delivery frequency.

I

Office

An office of twenty people uses between three and five kilos of coffee a month. Most of them drink something roasted long ago that has been sitting in a central warehouse since.

We deliver 250 g bags or larger units by agreement, at a fixed frequency or on demand. The coffee is roasted in small batches, and the roast date is stamped on every bag. You can see for yourself how fresh it is.

Suits offices, coworking spaces and meeting rooms where the coffee actually matters.

II

Café and Service

Guji Hambela is roasted on an omni profile, adapted to both espresso and filter. For a serving venue that means one lot covering the whole menu, instead of two different coffees on the counter.

We deliver in larger units for service use. Get in touch for pricing and delivery intervals — we adapt to how much you brew.

III

Retailer

Selling coffee on? Zewditu fits the range at delicatessens, specialty shops and farm shops that carry small, traceable producers.

The bags are fully labelled under Norwegian regulations with product designation, origin, net weight, shelf life and responsible business. The roast date is stamped by hand on each individual bag — something your customers can see with their own eyes.

IV

How We Do It

Get in touch. Tell us how much you use and how often. We reply with a price.

First delivery. Expect a few days from order to delivery.

After that. Fixed agreement or order on demand — you choose.
